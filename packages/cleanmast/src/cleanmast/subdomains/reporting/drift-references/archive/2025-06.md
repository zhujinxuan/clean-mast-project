## #3 — Air density computation source

- **Date**: 2025-06-25
- **Nature**: CONTENT
- **Status**: RESOLVED

### Context
The reporting subdomain's `AirDensitySection` computes air density from raw timeseries data (mean temperature, mean pressure, ideal gas law). The assessment reference reads `air_density = 1.238` from the `rep-ts-report.toml` config. This caused minor precision differences in height-extrapolated densities.

### Resolution
Air density subsection shall use the timeseries computation (current reporting behavior). Other subsections (wind_rose, temporal, etc.) shall use config data for their power density calculations.
